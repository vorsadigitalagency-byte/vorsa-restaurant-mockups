Our Story

A grill, a spice box, and one long table

Char & Clove opened with a simple question: why does a proper steak dinner and a good karahi have to happen in two different restaurants? We built one kitchen around both — open-flame grill on one side, a slow-cooked desi pass on the other.

Every dish still comes off that same pass today, plated with a little more care than the coal fire suggests, and served at a table meant to be shared.
