The short version

Started with one coal pit and a used scooter

Dhuaan began as a single grill stand in Karachi, built around one idea: BBQ shouldn't need a fancy dining room to taste right. Just real coal, real cuts, and someone who actually watches the fire.

That's still how the kitchen runs today — bigger menu, same pit-first mindset. Whether it's a seekh roll on the way home or a full steak dinner in, everything leaves the kitchen the same way it started: hot off the coals.
