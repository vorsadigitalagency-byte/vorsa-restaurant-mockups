Our Story

Built around a small kitchen and a long table.

Sufra began as an idea between three cooks who kept ending up at the same wholesale markets before dawn — for the best prawns, the ripest tomatoes, the freshest herbs. We opened this dining room to cook the way we cook at home: with patience, and without shortcuts.

The menu changes with the seasons because the markets do. Every table is set for guests who want to slow down for a few hours — no rush between courses, no music louder than conversation.

— The Kitchen at Sufra
